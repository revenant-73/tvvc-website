Register Your Team: Contact us or register through the Parent Dashboard to secure your spot once registration opens.

Tournament Format

Designed for competitive play with guaranteed full-day matches for all teams.

Pool Play Structure

  • 12 teams total per division
  • 3 pools of 4 teams each
  • Standard pool play format
  • Guaranteed full day of play for every team

Playoff Brackets

After pool play, teams advance to one of two playoff brackets:

  • Gold Bracket: Top 8 teams
  • Silver Bracket: Next 4 teams

All teams continue playing through to completion, ensuring every athlete gets multiple matches and a complete competitive experience.

Officiating & Staffing

Certified Officials

This is a recreational event—no certified officials will be provided.

TVVC Officiating

TVVC may provide R1 and R2 officials depending on availability. Final confirmation will be sent before the event.

Additional Staff

Line judges and scorekeepers will be provided by Century High School Girls Volleyball.
