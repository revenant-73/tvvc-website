# TVVC Summer Grass Volleyball Tournament Divisions

This document explains the suggested divisions for the TVVC Summer Grass Volleyball Series and the TVVC Family Challenge.

All events begin at **8:00 AM** and will be held at **Century High School on the grass field behind the school next to the baseball field**.

---

## Summer Grass Volleyball Series

### Event Dates

- **Summer Series #1:** June 27, 2026
- **Summer Series #2:** July 18, 2026

### Format

The Summer Series events are for players entering **7th through 12th grade**.

Teams will register for one of two divisions:

- **A Division**
- **B Division**

Cost is **$15 per player**.

---

## Summer Series Division Descriptions

### A Division — Competitive

The **A Division** is for experienced players who are ready for a faster, more competitive tournament environment.

This division is best for players who:

- Have club, high school, or strong middle school volleyball experience
- Are comfortable with faster rallies and stronger serving
- Can serve consistently
- Can communicate and play with some independence
- Want a more competitive grass volleyball experience

Suggested registration wording:

**A Division is for experienced players looking for a faster, more competitive grass tournament experience.**

---

### B Division — Developmental / Recreational Competitive

The **B Division** is for newer or developing players who want a fun, competitive environment with a little less speed and pressure.

This division is best for players who:

- Are newer to volleyball or still building confidence
- May have middle school, recreational, JV, or limited club experience
- Want competitive games without the pressure of the top division
- Are still learning how to play in grass volleyball situations
- Want a positive, lower-pressure tournament experience

Suggested registration wording:

**B Division is for newer or developing players who want a fun, competitive environment with a little less speed and pressure.**

---

## Important Note About Summer Series Divisions

Players should register for the division that best matches their current experience level.

Divisions are not strictly based on grade. A strong younger player may belong in A Division, while a newer older player may be a better fit for B Division.

TVVC may adjust teams or divisions if needed to create the best tournament experience.

---

# TVVC Family Challenge

## Event Date

- **Family Challenge:** August 8, 2026

## Format

The Family Challenge is a fun grass volleyball event for youth players and their families.

Each team includes:

- **One youth player age 18 or younger**
- **One parent or family member age 30 or older**

Teams will register for one of two divisions:

- **Competitive Family Division**
- **Fun Family Division**

---

## Family Challenge Division Descriptions

### Competitive Family Division

The **Competitive Family Division** is for teams that are ready to play hard, compete, and chase the win.

This division is best for teams where:

- The adult has volleyball or athletic experience
- The youth player has club, school, or strong playing experience
- Both players want a more serious competitive environment
- Rallies may be faster and more competitive
- The team wants to compete for the top finish

Suggested registration wording:

**Competitive Family Division is for teams ready to play hard, compete, and chase the win.**

---

### Fun Family Division

The **Fun Family Division** is for teams that want a relaxed, lighthearted tournament experience built around family, laughter, and playing together.

This division is best for teams where:

- The adult is newer, rusty, or mostly there for fun
- The youth player may be younger or less experienced
- The goal is good rallies, connection, and shared experience
- Winning matters less than playing together
- The team wants a fun, lower-pressure tournament environment

Suggested registration wording:

**Fun Family Division is for teams who want a relaxed, lighthearted tournament experience built around family, laughter, and playing together.**

---

## Important Note About Family Challenge Divisions

Teams should register for the division that best matches their experience level and goals for the event.

TVVC may adjust divisions if needed to create the best tournament experience.
