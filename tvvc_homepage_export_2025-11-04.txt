About TVVC
Our Story
Tualatin Valley Volleyball Club was founded in 2024 with a clear purpose: to make high-quality volleyball accessible, joyful, and rooted in what matters most—the growth of the athlete.
As a new coach at Century High School, our director recognized a gap in the local volleyball landscape. Too many families were priced out of club sports, or overwhelmed by demanding schedules and rigid training that left little room for joy. TVVC was born to change that.
We launched our first season in 2024–2025 with six teams and a commitment to do things differently:
- Affordability – We keep costs low so more athletes can play.
- Balance – Our time-conscious schedule minimizes travel and honors family life.
- Joyful Learning – Practices are grounded in play, exploration, and confidence-building.
Our mission goes beyond wins and rankings. We aim to spark a lifelong love of the game, nurture curiosity, and help each player grow into a confident, adaptable human being—on and off the court.

Our Core Values
These ten principles guide everything we do at TVVC—from how we design practices to how we communicate with athletes. They reflect our commitment to developing not just better volleyball players, but confident, resilient individuals.
1) Curiosity Over Certainty — We embrace questions, exploration, and learning through experience—because growth comes from the unknown.
2) Adaptability in Action — Our training environments prepare athletes to thrive in dynamic, unpredictable game situations—not just controlled drills.
3) Athlete Ownership — Players are active participants in their learning journey, empowered to reflect, make choices, and find their own solutions.
4) Connection Before Correction — We build trust and understanding first—because meaningful change only happens in safe, supportive relationships.
5) Play with Purpose — Every activity is rooted in the context of the game, helping athletes connect movement with intention.
6) Resilience Through Challenge — Struggle isn't failure—it's feedback. We welcome it as part of the growth process.
7) Freedom to Explore — We remove unnecessary constraints and let athletes test, adjust, and discover what works for them.
8) Authenticity Always — Coaches and players bring their whole selves. We don't fake intensity or force perfection—we show up real.
9) Joy in the Process — We believe learning is best fueled by enjoyment, laughter, and shared excitement.
10) Support with Standards — We hold high expectations, but meet athletes where they are—guiding with empathy, not control.

Making Club Volleyball Accessible for Everyone
At TVVC, we believe that every talented athlete deserves the opportunity to play club volleyball, regardless of their family's financial situation. We're committed to working with families to find solutions that make participation possible.

Financial Options
- Flexible Payment Plans — Customized schedules that work for your family's budget.
- Scholarship Opportunities — Need-based financial assistance for families who qualify.
- Fundraising Support — Guidance and opportunities for team and individual fundraising.
- Open Communication — Honest conversations to find creative solutions.
Our Promise: If your athlete is selected for a TVVC team, we will work with your family to ensure financial barriers don't prevent participation.
