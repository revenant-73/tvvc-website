Ignition Volleyball

Play. Learn. Love the Game.

Ignition is for 4th–6th graders to fall in love with volleyball. Kids want fun, friendship, and to be seen by adults who care. Ignition delivers all three—with games, teamwork, and the fundamentals.

Why Ignition?

Smaller courts, lower nets, lighter balls—these adjustments make the game accessible and fun. Every player touches the ball more, works with teammates in realistic situations, and builds real skills from day one.

What to Expect

We use games to teach and learn, because they're both fun and realistic. Athletes move quickly from skill demonstrations to small-sided games, exploring the concepts we're teaching through play. Our curriculum focuses on the fundamentals — serving, passing, setting, and attacking — through varied game formats that maximize touches, engagement, and decision-making opportunities.

Every practice is designed around what kids want: to have fun, play with friends, be noticed, and experience the joy of volleyball.

Learning Through Play

Three essential components in every Ignition session:

Fun

Kids want to have fun. Ignition centers joy and play in every session, making volleyball exciting and memorable.

Connection

Kids want to play with friends and peers. Small-sided games and team activities build friendships and belonging.

Recognition

Kids want adults to see them. Our coaches celebrate effort, progress, and participation, building confidence in every player.

PlayWorks Volleyball

Play. Learn. Grow.

PlayWorks is for 6th–10th graders who want to learn and improve without a full club commitment. Explore the game through fun, competitive play—building skills, creativity, and confidence along the way.

What to Expect

The first session of each week is devoted to introducing and refining core volleyball skills — serving, passing, setting, and attacking — through small-sided games that maximize touches, engagement, and decision-making opportunities.

The second session builds on those concepts with full-court play (4v4 or 6v6), featuring rotating challenges and themes that help each athlete and team learn to adapt, communicate, and compete with purpose.

Learning That Lasts

Three essential components in every PlayWorks session:

Autonomy

Athletes are given a voice and ownership in their learning process. Their input helps shape each day's focus, building confidence and buy-in.

Mastery

True mastery comes through exploration and persistence. Players discover how to respond to teammates, opponents, and new challenges within the game.

Purpose

Each athlete arrives with their own "why." Together, we connect those individual purposes into a shared drive to grow, compete, and have fun.
