Ignition Volleyball

Play. Learn. Fall in Love.

Ignition is an opportunity for 4th–6th graders to fall in love with the game of volleyball through play. We believe that at this age, the best way to learn is through joy, movement, and connection.

Why Ignition?

  • Kids want to have fun.
  • Kids want to play with friends and peers.
  • Kids want adults to see them.
  • Kids want to play.

✨ The Child at the Center

Ignition places the child at the center of the experience. Practices are built around fun, fast-moving games that encourage exploration, teamwork, and confidence. Instead of training young athletes on a full-size court and net, Ignition uses smaller courts, lower nets, and a lighter ball. These constraints better match how kids move, think, and learn, while setting them up for stronger long-term development.

💡 How Learning Happens

We use scoring and rule variations to teach the principles of volleyball through small-sided games, giving each athlete frequent touches and meaningful involvement. In our gym, games are how learning happens. Athletes move quickly from brief demonstrations into play, where they explore ideas, solve problems, and build a love for the sport.

6th–10th Grade

PlayWorks Volleyball

Play. Learn. Grow.

PlayWorks is an opportunity for 6th–10th graders to explore the game of volleyball through a variety of fun, competitive games. There will be a heavy focus on developing the skills of serving, passing, setting, and attacking.

Each week is designed to build both skill and game understanding through smaller-sided play. Early-week sessions emphasize skill development inside games that maximize touches and decision-making. Later-week sessions build toward more game-like complexity and problem solving.

Why PlayWorks?

  • Heavy focus on core skills
  • Game-based learning
  • Maximize touches & decisions
  • Low club commitment
  • Fun & competitive environment

🔍 What to Expect

PlayWorks emphasizes developing core volleyball skills through small-sided games that maximize touches and engagement. We focus on building both technical proficiency and game intelligence in a competitive, supportive environment.

🌱 Learning That Lasts

We believe in building autonomy, mastery, and purpose. Each session is designed to give athletes ownership of their learning while exploring the depths of the game.

🎯

Autonomy

Athletes are given a voice and ownership in their learning process. Their input helps shape each day's focus, building confidence and buy-in.

📈

Mastery

True mastery comes through exploration and persistence. Players discover how to respond to teammates, opponents, and new challenges within the game.

🤝

Purpose

Each athlete arrives with their own "why." Together, we connect those individual purposes into a shared drive to grow, compete, and have fun.
