Summer Camps

Movement. Competition. Real Decision-Making.

At TVVC, summer camps are built around representative play, small-sided games, and real decision-making. Athletes won’t spend their mornings standing in long drill lines. They’ll move, compete, solve problems, and learn how the game actually works.

  • Location – TVVC Facility
  • Time – 8:00am–12:00pm (Morning sessions only)
  • Cost – $185 per camp
  • Capacity – Maximum 20 athletes per camp

Registration Info

Public registration opens March 2026. Early enrollment will be offered to current TVVC families before public registration opens.

"Cost should never be the reason an athlete misses out. If finances are a barrier, please reach out — we will work something out."

June 15–17

6th–8th Grade Camp – Level 1

Who it’s for: Athletes entering 6th–8th grade new to organized volleyball.

Introduces foundational skills (serving, passing, setting, attacking) inside a team system through game-like activities.

June 22–24

Hitting Camp – Level 1

Who it’s for: Athletes developing approach, arm swing, timing, and coordination.

Focuses on building a strong attacking foundation within realistic game-like scenarios.

July 6–8

6th–8th Grade Camp – Level 2

Who it’s for: Athletes entering 6th–8th grade who have played school/club team.

Increased pace, longer rallies, and harder decisions. Sharpening the edge of technical and tactical skills.

July 13–15

Ignition Camp – 4th–6th Grade

Who it’s for: Athletes entering 4th–6th grade newer to volleyball.

Building real fundamentals while falling in love with the game through energetic, game-based activities.

July 20–22

Hitting Camp – Level 2

Who it’s for: Experienced hitters who want to elevate their attacking game.

Maximizing power and vertical jump while emphasizing mid-air decisions and reading the block.

Who it’s for: Athletes entering 6th–8th grade newer to organized volleyball.

A second opportunity to build strong fundamentals before fall seasons begin.

High School Tryout Prep Camp

Who it’s for: Athletes entering 9th–12th grade preparing for tryouts.

Build confidence with core systems, serve receive structure, and realistic scrimmage scenarios.

A second opportunity to sharpen readiness and build competitive clarity heading into tryouts.

Summer Clinics

Focused. High-Quality. Small-Group Training.

Clinics are designed for athletes looking to sharpen specific skills or gain extra touches without committing to a full camp week. One court, capped enrollment, and direct feedback.

  • Location – TVVC Facility
  • Cost – $30 per clinic
  • Format – 90-minute sessions
  • Capacity – 12 athletes per session

Clinic Format

Registration is à la carte—attend one or multiple. Each date offers three focused blocks:

  • Hitting – Footwork, timing, and shot selection.
  • Serving – Consistency, power, and strategic intent.
  • Defense / Serve Receive – Platform control and reading hitters.
