TUALATIN VALLEY VOLLEYBALL CLUB — BRAND & STYLE GUIDE (WEB VERSION)
Captured on: November 4, 2025

------------------------------------------------------------
🎨 COLOR PALETTE
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Teal — #00B4B3 — Buttons, headers, accent borders, hover states
Primary Coral — #FF6F61 — Highlights, call-to-action buttons, section dividers
Charcoal — #222222 — Primary text color, header/footer backgrounds
Cool Gray — #F3F3F3 — Background fill, alternate section color
White — #FFFFFF — Page background, cards, text on dark areas
Highlight Yellow (optional) — #FFD166 — Hover effects or accent marks

------------------------------------------------------------
🖋️ TYPOGRAPHY
------------------------------------------------------------
Headings (H1–H3): Montserrat or Poppins — Weight 600–700 — Modern, athletic feel
Body Text: Open Sans or Inter — Weight 400–500 — Readable, friendly
Accent / Quote Text: Playfair Display or DM Serif Display — Weight 500–600 — Used for quotes/mottos

Text Style Notes:
- Sentence-case headers for warmth
- Coral underline or bar for emphasis
- Short paragraphs (2–3 sentences max)

------------------------------------------------------------
🧱 LAYOUT & SPACING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Section structure: hero → about → values → teams → tryouts → footer
- Container width: max 1200px; padding 5–8%
- Alternate backgrounds: white ↔ light gray
- Hero section: full-width image with charcoal overlay (opacity 0.3), bold white text
- Buttons: rounded (12px radius), solid fill, white text, subtle shadow
  * Primary: #FF6F61
  * Secondary: #00B4B3
  * Hover: darken by ~8%

------------------------------------------------------------
📸 IMAGERY & GRAPHIC STYLE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Mood: authentic athlete moments, teamwork, laughter
- Tone: candid, warm, high contrast, slight film grain
- Filters: matte finish or vignette for community-gym vibe
- Design motif: black-ink zine overlays, halftone texture, hand-drawn elements

------------------------------------------------------------
💬 VOICE & TONE
------------------------------------------------------------
Warm & Authentic — “We believe learning is best fueled by joy and connection.”
Curious & Intelligent — “Every rep is an experiment in noticing, adapting, and committing.”
Inviting & Community-Focused — “Our doors are open to every player ready to grow.”

Avoid clichés (“No days off”), corporate language, or overused buzzwords.

------------------------------------------------------------
🧭 LOGO & ICON GUIDELINES
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary logo: “TVVC” stacked wordmark in teal with coral underline
Secondary mark: circle emblem or volleyball icon with text ring
Favicon: “TV” in coral on teal
Flat color only, with generous white space around marks

------------------------------------------------------------
⚙️ UI/UX DETAILS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Sticky navigation bar with coral accent line
- CTA buttons (“Register,” “Join Tryouts,” etc.) visible above the fold
- Smooth fade-in/slide-up motion (200ms)
- Accessibility:
  * Text contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1
  * Alt text for all images
  * Colors paired with text labels

------------------------------------------------------------
📅 Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
The TVVC website design should feel:
- Joyful but professional
- Modern but grounded
- Playful but intelligent
- Accessible and authentic

This concise guide supports consistent use of color, typography, and tone across all TVVC digital platforms.
