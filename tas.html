What Is TAS?

The Availability System (TAS) is our approach to preparing, strengthening, and calibrating the body so athletes stay healthy, explosive, and ready all season long — without grinding themselves into fatigue or stiffness.

TAS combines:

  • Low-load, high-frequency isometrics
  • Simple, consistent bodyweight strength
  • Asymmetric tendon-friendly loading
  • Movement calibration & awareness
  • Smart load management ("better is better")

TAS exists for one purpose:

To create athletes who are AVAILABLE — healthy, resilient, explosive, confident, and ready every day.

TAS is not a grind program. It's a readiness system designed to work with your season, not against it.

How to Use This Guide

  • Use the TAS Practice Warm-Up before every practice.
  • Follow the Weekly Isometric Schedule on non-practice days.
  • Use the Tournament Warm-Ups at events.
  • If you want to do extra work, follow the TAS Add-On Rules.
  • Do the Bodyweight Strength Program 2× per week on your own time.

Everything here is designed to support:

  • Joint health
  • Tendon resilience
  • Athletic posture
  • Movement clarity
  • Explosive ability
  • Consistent availability

TAS Add-On Guide (The No-Grind Rules)

Better is better. More is just more.

Rule 1 — Add, Don't Stack

If practice was hard → extra work must be light.

Rule 2 — The 10% Rule

Add only a small dose (≈10%), not a big one.

Rule 3 — Daily Add-On Limit

Max 10–15 minutes of extras per day.

Rule 4 — The 48-Hour Rule

If a joint is sore for >48 hours → back off and stick to Green Zone movement only.

Rule 5 — Tournament Week

Green Zone only (mobility, easy strength). Save your power.

Load Zones (TAS Intelligence System)

🟢 Green Zone — Always Safe
  • Mobility
  • Light stretching
  • Tibialis / soleus
  • Deep squat flow
  • Crawling / rocking
  • Light core
  • Easy walks
🟡 Yellow Zone — 2–3× / Week Max
  • Light plyos
  • Acceleration drills
  • ATG-style accessories
  • Low-volume skill work
🔴 Red Zone — Planned Only

Not for add-ons. For structured training ONLY.

  • Heavy lifting
  • High-volume jumps
  • HIIT
  • Long runs
  • Hard conditioning

Red Zone reduces readiness → use intentionally.

Multiple Sets of Isometrics (TAS Variation)

Best days for adding a second set:

  • Day 1
  • Day 3
  • Day 6
  • Day 7

How:

  • Set 1: 25s + 5s pulses
  • Rest 20–40s
  • Set 2: repeat

Movements worth doubling:

  • Tibialis
  • Soleus
  • KOT Lunge
  • External Rotation
  • Block Jump

Never double up the day before tournaments or on practice days.

Use NAC → Notice → Adapt → Commit.
