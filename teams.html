Age Group Teams

Our club teams compete in the CEVA region and train in Hillsboro. Each team is built around game-based, connected, and joyful learning.

🌱
Foundation Phase

12U–14U — Build the Foundation

Fun, supportive environment focused on building confidence and game understanding through play-based learning. We emphasize joy, teamwork, and the fundamentals of volleyball.

📈
Development Phase

15U–16U — Grow Through the Game

Developing skills while learning to read the game in real-time. We focus on decision-making and communication in a growth-first environment where development matters more than outcomes.

🏆
Competitive Phase

17U–18U — Lead with Confidence

A competitive space for athletes to elevate skills and leadership. Whether pursuing college volleyball or finishing their club journey, we focus on meaningful training and personal growth.

Tryout Quick Answers

What if my child can't attend tryouts?

Please contact us at info@tualatinvalleyvb.com — we can often arrange an alternate evaluation.

Do returning players need to try out?

Yes — every season begins fresh so we can fairly assess growth and new players.

How are players evaluated?

We assess learning potential, effort, attitude, and how players connect with others on the court. Technical skill is important, but we also value coachability and growth mindset.

When will I hear back about team placement?

We will contact every player that attends our tryouts within 24 hours after tryouts conclude.
