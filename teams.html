Age Group Teams

Competitive CEVA-region schedule with a development-first focus.

12U–14U — Build the Foundation

Fun, supportive environment focused on building confidence and game understanding through play-based learning. We emphasize joy, teamwork, and the fundamentals of volleyball.

15U–16U — Grow Through the Game

Developing skills while learning to read the game in real-time. We focus on decision-making and communication in a growth-first environment where development matters more than outcomes.

17U–18U — Lead with Confidence

A competitive space for athletes to elevate skills and leadership. Whether pursuing college volleyball or finishing their club journey, we focus on meaningful training and personal growth.

Tryout Information

Join Our Club Teams

We're not just looking for the most skilled players — we're looking for athletes who are curious, coachable, and excited to grow. At TVVC, our tryouts focus on learning potential, effort, attitude, and how players connect with others on the court. Registration is now open!
