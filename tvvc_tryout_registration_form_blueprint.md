# TVVC Tryout Registration Form Blueprint

## Purpose

A good tryout registration form should help the club do four things:

1. **Identify the athlete clearly.**
2. **Communicate with the right adults quickly.**
3. **Place the athlete in the correct age group and tryout group.**
4. **Protect the athlete, family, coaches, and club through clear permissions, medical awareness, and expectations.**

Do not collect information just because it might be interesting. Collect what helps you run a safer, clearer, better tryout.

---

# Recommended Form Structure

## Section 1 — Athlete Information

### Required Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Athlete first name | Short answer | Yes | Use legal or preferred first name consistently. |
| Athlete last name | Short answer | Yes | Needed for check-in, rosters, and offers. |
| Preferred name | Short answer | Recommended | Helpful if different from legal name. |
| Date of birth | Date | Yes | Needed for age group verification. |
| Current grade | Dropdown | Yes | 5th–12th, plus “Other.” |
| School currently attending | Short answer | Yes | Helpful for grouping, communication, and recruiting context. |
| Graduation year | Dropdown | Recommended | Especially useful for high school athletes. |
| Gender division registering for | Dropdown | Yes | Example: Girls, Boys, Other / contact club. |
| Athlete email | Email | Optional | Useful for older athletes, but parent email should remain primary. |
| Athlete phone | Phone | Optional | Use only if age-appropriate and parent-approved. |

### Recommended Notes

- Use **date of birth** rather than asking families to self-select age group only.
- Let the form calculate or confirm the correct age group later if your platform allows it.
- If the player uses a preferred name, that should appear on check-in sheets and evaluator cards.

## Section 2 — Parent / Guardian Information

### Required Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Primary parent/guardian first name | Short answer | Yes | Main contact. |
| Primary parent/guardian last name | Short answer | Yes | Main contact. |
| Primary email | Email | Yes | Main tryout and offer communication. |
| Primary phone | Phone | Yes | Needed for urgent communication. |
| Can this number receive texts? | Yes/No | Yes | Useful for last-minute tryout updates. |
| Secondary parent/guardian name | Short answer | Recommended | Helpful but not mandatory. |
| Secondary email | Email | Recommended | Useful if multiple households are involved. |
| Secondary phone | Phone | Recommended | Useful for urgent communication. |
| Household / custody communication notes | Paragraph | Optional | Only ask if needed. Keep wording neutral. |

### Recommended Wording

> Please list the adult who should receive primary communication about tryouts, offers, payments, and scheduling.

---

## Section 3 — Emergency Contact

### Required Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Emergency contact name | Short answer | Yes | Should be available during tryouts. |
| Relationship to athlete | Short answer | Yes | Parent, guardian, grandparent, family friend, etc. |
| Emergency phone | Phone | Yes | Must be reachable during tryouts. |
| Alternate emergency phone | Phone | Recommended | Optional but useful. |

### Recommended Note

If the emergency contact is the same as the primary parent/guardian, allow families to select “same as above.”

---

## Section 4 — Medical and Safety Information

### Required Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Does the athlete have any medical condition coaches should know about? | Yes/No | Yes | If yes, open follow-up text box. |
| Medical condition notes | Paragraph | Conditional | Keep focused on participation safety. |
| Allergies | Paragraph | Yes | Include food, medication, insect, latex, etc. “None” allowed. |
| Carries inhaler, EpiPen, or medication? | Checkbox | Yes | Include “No,” “Inhaler,” “EpiPen,” “Other.” |
| Medication instructions during tryouts | Paragraph | Conditional | Do not ask for unnecessary medical history. |
| Current injury or activity restriction? | Yes/No | Yes | If yes, ask for details. |
| Concussion history or current concussion restrictions? | Yes/No | Recommended | Useful for safety awareness. |
| Permission to provide basic first aid | Checkbox acknowledgement | Yes | Parent/guardian consent. |
| Permission to seek emergency medical care if guardian cannot be reached | Checkbox acknowledgement | Yes | Parent/guardian consent. |

### Recommended Wording

> Please share only information that would help TVVC coaches respond appropriately during tryouts. This form is not asking for a full medical history.

### Do Not Collect Unless Absolutely Necessary

- Social Security number
- Full insurance policy information
- Complete medical history
- Copies of medical cards
- Diagnosis details that are not relevant to safe participation

## Section 5 — Volleyball Background

### Required / Recommended Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Years playing volleyball | Dropdown | Yes | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4+. |
| Previous club experience | Yes/No | Yes | Opens follow-up if yes. |
| Previous club name(s) | Short answer | Conditional | Helps understand playing background. |
| Previous team level | Dropdown | Optional | Local, regional, national, school only, unsure. |
| School team experience | Checkboxes | Recommended | Middle school, freshman, JV2, JV, varsity, none. |
| Primary position played | Checkboxes | Recommended | Setter, outside, middle, opposite, libero/DS, unsure. |
| Secondary positions | Checkboxes | Optional | Avoid locking athletes into one role. |
| Position interested in exploring | Checkboxes | Recommended | Useful for player voice and grouping. |
| Dominant hand | Dropdown | Recommended | Right, left, both/ambidextrous. |
| Approximate height | Short answer | Optional | Helpful but should not drive early judgment. |
| Uniform jersey size | Dropdown | Recommended | Useful if offers are made quickly. |

### Recommended Note

Do not make the form feel like the athlete is being sorted before tryouts. Use language like:

> This helps us organize tryout groups. It does not lock the athlete into a position or team.

## Section 6 — Age Group and Tryout Session

### Required Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Age group registering for | Dropdown | Yes | 12U, 13U, 14U, 15U, 16U, 17U, 18U, Unsure. |
| Athlete date of birth confirmation | Checkbox | Yes | Family confirms DOB is accurate. |
| Tryout session attending | Dropdown / checkboxes | Yes | Use actual tryout dates and times. |
| Request to try out for older age group? | Yes/No | Optional | If yes, explain policy. |
| Sibling also trying out? | Yes/No | Optional | Useful for family communication. |

### Recommended Wording

> If you are unsure which age group your athlete belongs in, select “Unsure” and we will verify before tryouts.

---

## Section 7 — Availability and Commitment Fit

This section protects the club from making offers to families who cannot reasonably participate.

### Required / Recommended Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Can the athlete attend the full tryout? | Yes/No | Yes | If no, open explanation box. |
| Known conflicts during club season | Paragraph | Yes | Ask about school sports, travel, recurring conflicts, religious/family commitments. |
| Other winter/spring sports or activities | Checkboxes + short answer | Recommended | Basketball, softball, track, soccer, dance, etc. |
| Expected availability for practices | Dropdown / checkboxes | Recommended | Example: most weeknights, limited weeknights, unsure. |
| Expected availability for weekend tournaments | Dropdown | Recommended | Yes, mostly, limited, unsure. |
| Will the athlete be trying out for other clubs? | Yes/No/Prefer not to say | Optional | Useful for offer timelines; do not make it punitive. |
| If offered a spot, how quickly can family respond? | Dropdown | Recommended | Same day, 24 hours, 48 hours, need more conversation. |

### Recommended Wording

> We do not expect families to have every date solved today. We do need to know about major conflicts that could affect team placement or season commitment.

---

## Section 8 — Player Voice

This is where the form can reflect TVVC’s actual coaching values. Keep this short. Do not turn it into homework.

### Recommended Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| What is one thing you want coaches to know about you? | Paragraph | Recommended | Player answers if possible. |
| What part of volleyball do you enjoy most? | Paragraph | Optional | Helps coaches connect. |
| What is one area you want to grow in this season? | Paragraph | Recommended | Helps frame learning. |
| Choose one small tryout goal | Multiple choice | Recommended | See options below. |
| How do you usually respond when you make mistakes? | Multiple choice + optional note | Optional | Good coaching context, not evaluation. |

### Small Tryout Goal Options

- Call the ball earlier.
- Serve with a clear target.
- Attack with a plan.
- Reset faster after mistakes.
- Help organize teammates.
- Stay brave when the game gets messy.
- Notice what is happening before I move.
- Other: _______

### Recommended Wording

> Tryouts are not only about showing what you can already do. They are also about showing how you learn, compete, communicate, and respond.

## Section 9 — Parent / Guardian Expectations

### Recommended Fields

| Field | Type | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Parent understands tryout results may include placement, waitlist, or no offer | Checkbox | Yes | Important expectation-setter. |
| Parent understands team placement is based on multiple factors | Checkbox | Yes | Skill, role fit, commitment, age group, roster needs. |
| Parent understands playing time is not guaranteed equally | Checkbox | Recommended | Better here than after offers. |
| Parent agrees to respectful communication with coaches, athletes, and families | Checkbox | Yes | Sets conduct expectations early. |
| Parent has reviewed estimated season cost | Checkbox | Yes | Avoids offer-day confusion. |
| Parent has reviewed expected tournament/travel model | Checkbox | Yes | Especially important if TVVC emphasizes local/minimal travel. |

### Recommended Wording

> Team placement is not a ranking of athlete worth. It is a roster decision based on age group, skill, role fit, commitment, coach evaluation, and team needs.

## Section 11 — Waivers, Releases, and Permissions

Use your actual legal language here. The form should include these acknowledgement categories.

### Required Acknowledgements

| Acknowledgement | Required? | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Participation waiver / assumption of risk | Yes | Volleyball involves risk of injury. |
| Emergency medical authorization | Yes | Permission to seek care if needed. |
| Basic first aid permission | Yes | Minor injuries, ice, bandage, etc. |
| Concussion / removal-from-play acknowledgement | Recommended | Especially if your state/league requires it. |
| Communicable illness agreement | Optional | Stay home if sick, notify club if relevant. |
| Photo/video release | Recommended | Include opt-out option if possible. |
| Communication permission | Yes | Email/text/Spond/team app if used. |
| Code of conduct acknowledgement | Yes | Athlete and parent expectations. |
| Data/privacy acknowledgement | Recommended | Explain how information will be used. |

### Photo / Video Release Options

Recommended structure:

- Yes, TVVC may use photos/videos of my athlete for club communication and promotion.
- Yes, but only for internal/team communication.
- No, please do not use identifiable photos/videos of my athlete.

### Data Use Wording

> Information submitted through this form will be used for tryout organization, communication, safety, evaluation, team placement, and club administration. TVVC will not sell this information.

## Section 13 — Tryout Logistics

This section can be displayed before submission or included in the confirmation email.

### Information to Include

- Tryout date and time
- Arrival/check-in time
- Facility address
- Parking instructions
- What to bring:
  - Water bottle
  - Athletic clothing
  - Non-marking court shoes
  - Knee pads if the athlete uses them
  - Any required paperwork
  - Positive, competitive, learning-ready mindset
- What not to bring:
  - Jewelry that cannot be worn during play
  - Food/drink on court other than water if facility rules require it
- Parent viewing policy
- Expected end time
- Offer timeline
- How families will be contacted after tryouts

### Recommended Confirmation Message

> Thank you for registering. Please arrive early enough to check in, get your tryout number, put on shoes/knee pads, and be ready when the session begins. We recommend arriving 20–30 minutes before the scheduled start time.

# Recommended Field Order for the Actual Form

Use this order to reduce confusion and abandonment:

1. Athlete information
2. Parent/guardian information
3. Emergency contact
4. Age group and tryout session
5. Volleyball background
6. Availability and conflicts
7. Medical and safety information
8. Player voice questions
9. Parent expectations
10. Financial acknowledgement
11. Waivers and permissions
12. Final confirmation

The medical section can come earlier if your platform uses conditional logic well, but many families prefer to get basic registration details done first.

# Suggested Form Copy

## Opening Description

> Welcome to TVVC tryout registration. This form helps us place athletes in the correct tryout group, communicate with families, prepare for medical or safety needs, and understand each athlete a little better before they walk into the gym.
>
> Please complete the form carefully. If you are unsure about age group, position, or availability, choose the closest answer and add a note.

## Player Voice Intro

> TVVC coaches want to know more than your position and experience. We also want to know how you learn, compete, communicate, and respond when the game gets messy.

## Waiver Intro

> A parent or legal guardian must complete this section. By submitting this form, you confirm that the information is accurate and that you have reviewed the participation expectations, medical permissions, and club policies listed below.

## Final Confirmation Checkbox

> I confirm that the information in this registration is accurate to the best of my knowledge, and I understand that TVVC may use this information for tryout organization, safety, evaluation, communication, and team placement.

# Recommended “Do Not Overdo It” Rule

A tryout form should not become a 30-minute essay.

Target completion time:

- **8–12 minutes** for a normal family
- **15 minutes max** if payment and waiver language are included

If the form takes longer than that, families will rush it, abandon it, or give lower-quality answers.

---

# Best Next Build

Create the form in this order:

1. Build the required registration fields.
2. Add conditional logic for medical details.
3. Add player voice questions.
4. Add payment and waiver sections.
5. Create the confirmation email.
6. Export responses to a spreadsheet or evaluation app.
7. Assign tryout numbers from the response sheet before check-in.

---

# Final Recommendation

The strongest version of this form should feel like TVVC:

- Clear enough for parents.
- Human enough for players.
- Protective enough for the club.
- Useful enough for coaches.
- Short enough that families actually complete it carefully.

The form should not just collect names. It should start the relationship.
