# TVVC Website Design Roadmap (2026 Aesthetic)

This document outlines the 6-step roadmap to transition the TVVC website from a standard slick/glassmorphic design to a high-energy, tactile, and human-centric aesthetic.

## 1. Expressive Typography
**Objective**: Use typography as the main visual element rather than just text.
- **Implementation**: Introduced the **Syne** variable font.
- **Key Features**: Massive scale, high-impact headings, and tighter tracking (`tracking-tighter`) to create a "voice" for the brand.

## 2. Bold Color
**Objective**: Use high-contrast, energetic colors to match the intensity of volleyball.
- **Implementation**: Saturated the brand palette.
- **Key Features**: Electric gradients (teal to cyan), high-opacity background blobs, and saturated card accents.

## 3. Human Craft
**Objective**: Move away from "polished AI slickness" toward analog, tactile details.
- **Implementation**: Added film grain and physical collage elements.
- **Key Features**: 
    - Global SVG grain overlay.
    - Hand-drawn SVG underlines.
    - "Printed photo" effects with tape and rotation.
    - Paper texture overlays on cards.

## 4. Motion as Identity
**Objective**: Use animation to guide attention and create personality, not just decoration.
- **Planned**: 
    - Kinetic type (text that moves or transforms on scroll).
    - Scroll-triggered parallax on tactile elements (tape, photos).
    - Liquid-style transitions between sections.

## 5. Tactile Maximalism
**Objective**: Create "controlled chaos" through layering and asymmetry.
- **Planned**: 
    - Overlapping elements (text over images, images over cards).
    - Asymmetric grid layouts that break the standard container lines.
    - Complex depth with multi-layered textures.

## 6. Fast & Accessible Aesthetic
**Objective**: Ensure the heavy design remains performant and inclusive.
- **Planned**: 
    - Performance audit of SVG filters and animations.
    - WCAG contrast checks on bold color combinations.
    - Reduced motion options for accessibility.
